Why not on the Play Store?

Google doesn't allow apps that save others' videos for offline viewing, so — like most video downloaders — Snapload is distributed directly here instead. The app checks this page for updates on its own, so it stays current without an app-store account.

Privacy

Snapload collects anonymous usage data (things like which platforms and video qualities are popular) to improve the app — never anything that identifies you personally.
